# Aesop UAE: Independent CRO Audit

**Type:** Independent strategy exercise | **Platform:** Shopify Plus | **Market:** UAE (MENA)

> **Disclaimer:** This is an independent CRO audit and strategy exercise based on public UX analysis of Aesop's Shopify Plus storefront. All hypotheses, test designs, and projected uplifts are analytical, not results from a live client engagement. Aesop is not affiliated with this work.

## Overview

A structured Conversion Rate Optimization audit of Aesop's Shopify Plus storefront for the UAE market. Starting from public UX inspection, behavioral pattern benchmarking, and industry data, the exercise maps the full funnel friction landscape and produces a prioritized A/B test roadmap with projected impact estimates.

| Metric | Projection |
|---|---|
| Overall CVR uplift (mobile) | **+22%** (combined tests) |

*Projections grounded in Baymard Institute research, Shopify Plus industry benchmarks, and comparable luxury eCommerce implementations.*

## Scope

**01: Funnel Audit & Diagnostic**
- UX inspection across 6 funnel stages (homepage through checkout)
- Mobile viewport and browser chrome analysis
- Competitive benchmark: Byredo, Diptyque, Le Labo on Shopify Plus
- Friction mapping: navigation, PDP trust, cart abandonment drivers, paid traffic cold landing

**02: Hypotheses & Prioritization**
- 14 friction points identified, scored via ICE framework (Impact, Confidence, Ease)
- 4 hypotheses elevated to test roadmap
- Roadmap structured around mobile-first, new visitor segment

**03: A/B Test Designs**
- Sticky CTA bar on PDP (mobile): projected +30–35% add-to-cart
- Cart drawer trust signals: projected +20–28% cart-to-checkout
- Paid social trust banner on PDP (UTM-targeted): projected +40–60% CVR
- Guest checkout priority: projected +8–12% checkout completion

**04: Analysis Approach**
GA4 segmentation strategy, Hotjar heatmap framework, Convert.com test setup, statistical significance methodology (min. 95%, pre-calculated sample sizes)

## Key Learnings

1. **Mobile-first means interaction-first.** Browser chrome creates invisible friction that standard analytics won't surface: session recording is non-negotiable on luxury Shopify mobile.
2. **Luxury buyers need permission to trust.** In MENA markets, the biggest CRO wins happen before the checkout step, not inside it.
3. **Traffic source segmentation is the most underused lever on Shopify.** A minimal UTM-targeted trust layer for cold paid traffic is the highest-ROI intervention in this roadmap.
