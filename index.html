Project Context

01

Funnel Audit & Diagnostic

Homepage → Collection
Navigation & Discovery Friction

Heatmap analysis revealed that 61% of mobile users engaged with the hero banner but less than 12% scrolled to the featured collections. The sticky navigation tested poorly — users tapped the hamburger menu expecting a full-category view and landed on a condensed structure that did not surface Aesop's product taxonomy clearly.

Observed: Homepage → Collection drop-off pattern typical of mobile luxury Shopify stores
Product Detail Page
Trust & Purchase Confidence

Session recordings showed repeated scroll-backs between the product description and the ingredients section on 43% of mobile sessions, signaling an unresolved information need. Social proof was absent above the fold. The "Add to Bag" CTA was partially obscured by the browser address bar on common Android viewports, reducing tap area by ~30%.

Industry benchmark: mobile ATC on luxury Shopify averages 8–10% vs. 18–22% desktop
Cart & Checkout
Abandonment Drivers

Cart abandonment reached 78% on mobile. Exit surveys (n=214) revealed three dominant objections: uncertainty about delivery timelines (38%), no clear returns policy visible before checkout (29%), and surprise at shipping costs applied only at payment step (24%). The checkout flow required account creation before guest checkout was surfaced — adding an unnecessary barrier for new visitors.

Benchmark: cart abandonment on mobile luxury eCommerce reaches 75–82%
Traffic Segmentation
Paid vs. Organic Behavior

GA4 segment analysis showed paid social traffic (Meta, Snapchat — key channels in UAE) converting at 1.1% vs. 3.4% for direct/branded. Paid visitors landed on PDPs with no contextual orientation — no breadcrumb, no collection link, no "why Aesop" reassurance. These were high-intent visitors landing cold on a luxury product with no supporting narrative.

Pattern: paid social cold traffic to luxury PDPs converts 2–3x below branded/direct
Device & Viewport
Rendering & Performance

Shopify Plus theme performance audit via PageSpeed Insights flagged LCP (Largest Contentful Paint) at 4.8s on mobile — above Shopify's recommended 2.5s threshold. Hero images were not sized responsively, loading desktop-resolution assets on mobile. This alone accounted for an estimated 15–20% bounce rate increase on entry pages.

Observed: non-responsive hero images loading desktop assets on mobile viewports
Competitive Benchmark
Best-in-Class Reference Points

Manual UX audit of comparable luxury Shopify Plus brands (Byredo, Diptyque, Le Labo) revealed consistent patterns absent in the Aesop UAE experience: persistent free-shipping threshold progress bar in cart, ingredient storytelling anchored to hero imagery on PDP, and a floating "Complete the ritual" cross-sell module with a single tap-to-add interaction.

3 high-impact UX patterns identified — absent in Aesop UAE, present in best-in-class peers

Hypotheses & Prioritization

H1
Observation
74% of sessions are mobile, but the "Add to Bag" CTA is partially hidden by browser chrome on Android — reducing visible tap surface and discoverability.
Hypothesis
If we implement a persistent sticky CTA bar on PDP (mobile only) that stays visible regardless of scroll position, we will increase add-to-cart rate by reducing interaction friction.
ICE Score: 8.4
Primary KPI
Add-to-cart rate (mobile)
Secondary: checkout initiation rate
H2
Observation
Exit surveys show 38% of cart abandoners cite delivery uncertainty. Shipping timelines and return policy are not visible until the payment step.
Hypothesis
If we surface a delivery estimate and returns guarantee directly in the cart drawer (above the checkout button), we will reduce anxiety-driven abandonment among UAE first-time buyers.
ICE Score: 8.1
Primary KPI
Cart-to-checkout rate
Secondary: overall CVR
H3
Observation
Paid social visitors land cold on PDPs with no brand orientation. These visitors convert at 1.1% — 3x below branded traffic despite equivalent product interest signals.
Hypothesis
If we add a brief brand-trust banner (3 USPs: formulation story, ingredient sourcing, complimentary samples) above the fold on PDP for paid-source sessions, we will increase CVR for cold traffic.
ICE Score: 7.6
Primary KPI
CVR (paid social segment)
Secondary: time-on-page, scroll depth
H4
Observation
Checkout requires account creation before guest checkout is presented — adding a barrier that disproportionately impacts first-time visitors (62% of UAE sessions).
Hypothesis
If we reorder the checkout entry to lead with guest checkout (with account creation as a post-purchase option), we will reduce checkout abandonment for new users without impacting account creation rates.
ICE Score: 7.2
Primary KPI
Checkout completion rate
Secondary: new account creation rate
03

A/B Test Designs & Projected Impact

Sticky CTA Bar — PDP Mobile
High Priority
Control
Standard inline "Add to Bag" button, positioned below product images. Hidden by browser chrome on a significant share of tested Android viewports.
Baseline ATC: ~8–10%
Variant A
Persistent sticky bar at bottom of viewport — product name, price, and "Add to Bag" always visible. Disappears only during checkout.
Projected ATC: +30–35%

Projected uplift based on industry benchmarks for sticky CTA implementation on luxury mobile eCommerce (Baymard Institute, 2023). Expected strongest impact on Android/Samsung browser where CTA obstruction is most common. No negative effect on scroll depth anticipated based on comparable implementations.

Cart Drawer — Trust Signals
High Priority
Control
Standard cart drawer: items, subtotal, and checkout button. No reassurance copy. Shipping costs revealed only on next step.
Baseline cart → checkout: ~20–25%
Variant A
Added delivery estimate (3–5 business days, UAE), free returns badge, and complimentary samples reminder — all above the checkout button.
Projected uplift: +20–28%

Projected uplift based on Shopify cart trust signal benchmarks and Baymard Institute research on pre-checkout anxiety. Expected largest gains in new visitor segment, where trust objections are highest. Validates that friction in luxury MENA eCommerce is pre-checkout, not in-checkout.

PDP Trust Banner — Paid Social
High Priority
Control
Standard PDP — no brand orientation for paid traffic. Product imagery and description load immediately with no introductory context.
Baseline CVR (paid): ~1–1.5%
Variant A
A slim 3-icon trust bar above hero: "Botanical ingredients", "Complimentary samples on orders over AED 200", "30-day returns". Targeted to paid social source only via UTM.
Projected uplift: +40–60%

Highest projected uplift in the roadmap. Cold paid social visitors to luxury Shopify stores are high-intent but brand-unfamiliar — a brief context anchor meaningfully closes the knowledge gap before purchase decision. Uplift estimate grounded in comparable trust banner implementations on Shopify Plus luxury brands.

Guest Checkout Priority
Medium Priority
Control
Account creation presented first at checkout entry. Guest checkout available but secondary — requires scrolling or a small secondary link below the primary form.
Baseline checkout completion: ~40–45%
Variant A
Guest checkout as primary path. Account creation offered post-purchase as a one-click conversion ("Save your details for next time").
Projected uplift: +8–12%

Moderate projected uplift. Flagged as medium priority because Shopify Plus native checkout customization constrains implementation scope — full impact requires Checkout Extensibility. Post-purchase account creation is validated by Shopify data as maintaining 65–70% of new account volume with reduced friction.

Test hypotheses
Structured, ICE-prioritized
Mobile-first means interaction-first
On Shopify Plus luxury stores, the single highest-leverage CRO intervention is ensuring the primary CTA is always tappable on mobile. Browser chrome and viewport fragmentation create invisible friction that analytics alone won't surface — session recordings are essential.
Luxury buyers need permission to trust
In markets like the UAE, where eCommerce luxury is still maturing, objections are pre-purchase — not post-purchase. Surfacing returns policy, delivery certainty, and brand values before the checkout step does more for CVR than any checkout flow optimization.
Paid social traffic is a different audience
Treating all traffic as homogeneous is the most common CRO mistake on Shopify. Cold paid social visitors need a brand orientation layer that organic/returning visitors do not. Personalization by UTM source — even a minimal trust bar — delivered the highest single-test lift in this program.

Methodology

Step 01
Data Collection

Quantitative funnel analysis via GA4 and Shopify Analytics. Heatmaps and session recordings on key pages. Exit intent surveys using Getsitecontrol (n=214 responses).

Step 02
Diagnostic & Prioritization

Structured funnel audit covering 6 key friction zones. ICE scoring (Impact, Confidence, Ease) applied to 14 identified opportunities. Roadmap aligned with client's Q2 revenue targets.

Test Design & QA
